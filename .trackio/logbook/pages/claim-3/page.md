# Claim 3 — Poisson transformations

## Direct result

**VERIFIED.** The `φ_λ` realization transformations agree with an independent
exact rational checker in 24/24 cases. Four allocation-then-subsampling cases
verify that the rules compose in one PLD framework. Mutated transformations are
rejected in 24/24 cases.

subsampling lowers epsilon would not identify whether the transformations were
correct.
